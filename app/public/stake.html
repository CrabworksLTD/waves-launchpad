Staking

Launch a pair and the token's trading fees can flow to the collection's holders. Holders burn the token to activate their NFTs — no unstaking, no vault holding your assets — and claim rewards in whatever the creator chose: SOL, USDC, or tokenized stocks and gold.

1Burn to activate

Burn the amount the creator set to switch an NFT on. The token is gone for good; your NFT stays in your wallet. Selling it ends its activation.

2Fees accrue

Every trade of the paired token pays its creator share into the reward vault, forwarded there by the keeper on a schedule.

3Claim

Fees split equally across every activated NFT. Claim whenever — what you've earned is yours, tracked on chain.
