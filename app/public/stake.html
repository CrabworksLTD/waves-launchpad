Staking

Launch a pair and the token's trading fees can flow to the collection's holders. Holders burn the token to activate their NFTs — no unstaking, no vault holding your assets — and claim rewards in whatever the creator chose: SOL, USDC, or tokenized stocks and gold.

In development. The staking program is being built and will be audited before it touches real money. Everything below is the real interface, parked until then. COMING
1Burn to activate

Send the paired token to the program — it burns, permanently, and your NFT's tier rises with the amount. Selling the NFT ends its activation.

2Fees accrue

Every trade of the paired token pays its creator share into the reward vault. The keeper converts it to the reward asset on a schedule.

3Claim

Rewards split across activated NFTs by tier weight. Claim whenever — what you've earned is yours, tracked on chain.
