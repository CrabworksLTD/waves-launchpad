Deploy the swap router

What lets the rewards keeper pay holders the asset their creator picked, instead of ETH. Done once.

Why this contract exists. A Uniswap V4 swap only happens inside a lock the PoolManager hands out, and it hands it back by calling a contract. The keeper is a wallet, so it cannot receive that call — which is why it pays ETH to everyone today, whatever they chose. This is the piece that closes that gap.

It holds nothing, and nobody owns it. No owner, no withdrawal, no upgrade, and no storage that survives a call. Anyone may use it — they spend their own ETH and name their own recipient — so there is nothing in it to steal and nothing that can be stranded in it.

Not audited. Fork-tested against the real V4 pools on this chain, which is not the same thing.
