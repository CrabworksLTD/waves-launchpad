The editor

A real drawing tool, not just an upload box.

Pixel or paint

Pixel mode snaps to a grid for crisp, blocky art. Brush mode gives you a soft pencil, marker, chalk and airbrush — and it follows the pressure of a stylus if you draw on a tablet.

See the whole character

While you draw a hat, the rest of the character sits faintly underneath — so it lands on the head instead of floating above it.

One hat, six colors

Draw a hat once, then add colorways. Each one is a real drawing you can keep tweaking, so it can have a gradient or a pattern rather than just a hue slider dragged across it.

Trace over anything

Drop a photo or a sketch behind the canvas and draw on top of it. It never ends up in your collection.

Rules that stop the weird ones

No hoods over long hair, no glasses under a helmet. Set it once and every bad pairing gets thrown out and rerolled.

Your work stays yours

Save the whole project — art and all — as one file on your machine, and open it again whenever. Nothing is held on a server.
